How an ad gets made

  1. Script and shot list. One idea, written as timed shots with lens, action and sound.
  2. Reference sheets. Cast, wardrobe, props and locations as image sheets so faces and products stay the same in every shot.
  3. Keyframes. First and last frames for the hard shots, checked before any video credits are spent.
  4. Drafts at 480p. Cheap passes to lock motion and timing, then retakes only for the shots that broke.
  5. Edit to the music. Cuts placed on the beat, voice and stems mixed, colour matched across shots.
  6. 4K finish. Topaz upscale and a clean master, delivered in 16:9, 9:16 or both.
